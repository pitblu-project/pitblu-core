# Third-party notices

No third-party source code has been copied or substantially adapted into this
repository. Runtime and development packages retain their own licences and
copyright notices in their installed distributions.

The [dependency audit](docs/dependency-audit.md) records reviewed licence and
platform evidence. Its version-specific results describe the v0.9.0 baseline;
current release dependencies are declared in [pyproject.toml](pyproject.toml)
and are checked by CI. The project's MIT licence does not relicense dependencies.

The Weber iGrill protocol facts were independently implemented from the sources
and reviewed revisions in [provenance](docs/provenance.md).
